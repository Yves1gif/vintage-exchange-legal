VINTAGE EXCHANGE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

In short: no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking. Your game stays on your phone. Ads appear only when you tap “Watch a short ad”, and Google, who serves them, may process some data for that — with your consent where the law requires it.

Who we are

Vintage Exchange is made by Yves Flückiger, Switzerland. Questions about privacy: yvesflueckiger@icloud.com.

Your game

Your estate, cellar, trades and settings are saved only on your device. We run no servers for the game and never receive your game data. Deleting the app deletes it.

The name you enter at the start is stored on your device only and shown only to you.

Location (optional)

To suggest the wine region nearest to you, the app first guesses from your device’s time zone, on the device. Only if you tap “Use my location” does iOS ask for permission; the app then reads one approximate position, uses it on the device to sort regions and pick your home hub city, and discards it. Your location is never stored and never sent anywhere.

Ads

The only ads are optional rewarded videos. Nothing in the game requires them; they unlock cosmetic items such as label designs. They are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

When you choose to watch an ad, Google may collect and process data such as your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, an app-specific identifier, and how you interact with the ad, in order to deliver, measure and protect ads. The app does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choices before any ad is requested. You can change them at any time in Network › Settings › Ad privacy choices.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

No real money, no real wine

Vintage Exchange is a game. Wine, money and markets in it are simulated. You cannot buy, sell or receive real wine or money through the app, and there are no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and, where it applies, the GDPR, you may ask what data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Because we do not receive personal data from the app, there is usually nothing we hold; for ad data, use the privacy choices above or contact Google. Write to yvesflueckiger@icloud.com for any request. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Switzerland: the FDPIC).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
