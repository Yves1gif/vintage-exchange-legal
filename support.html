Build a winery. Trade the vintage.

Something wrong, an idea, or a question? Write to yvesflueckiger@icloud.com — usually answered within two days. A screenshot and your iOS version help.

How do I make money early on?

Your first reds spend 18 months in oak. Sell some inherited wine on the Market, and sell part of the coming harvest En Primeur: cash now, the buyer carries the weather.

Why did my net worth drop?

Upkeep, interest and picking cost money before the wine is sold. The crop on the vine counts more as the season goes on; a frost or hail strike lowers it.

Can I play faster?

Tap the speed button in the time bar (1×, 2×, 4× weeks per second). Settings let the game pick harvest timing and cellar choices for you.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you tap “Watch a short ad” in the Cellar Door, and they only unlock cosmetic items. Change ad privacy choices in Network › Settings.

Is any of this real wine or money?

No. Everything is simulated. There are no in-app purchases.

How do I start over?

Network › Settings › Start a new game.
